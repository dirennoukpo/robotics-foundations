               As not all matrices represent scaling, it is not always possible to find
            real eigenvalues. In particular, a rotation matrix has no real eigenvalues
            for angle of rotation, θ = 0. Another point to note is that in this example
            the eigenvectors were at right angles to each other. This is only true for
            symmetric matrices (which we had in this case).
