                                 graph of this function can be represented on the page of a book or a

                                 function is constant.
                              2. A function of two variables f (x, y) has two first-order partial deriva-
                                 tives. The partial derivative with respect to x is found by considering
                                 y to be constant and then differentiating with respect to x only. The
                                 partial derivative with respect to y is found by considering x to be a
                                 constant and differentiating with respect to y only.
