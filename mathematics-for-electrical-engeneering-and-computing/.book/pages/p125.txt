                                            sin(t) and tan(t) are odd functions, while cos(t) is an even function.
                                            This can be expressed by

                                            sin(−t) = − sin(t)
                                            cos(−t) = cos(t)
                                            tan(−t) = − tan(t)

                                            sin(t) and cos(t) are periodic with period 2π and tan(t) is periodic
                                            with period π . This can be expressed by

                                            sin(t + 2π n) = sin(t)
                                            cos(t + 2π n) = cos(t)
                                             tan(t + π n) = tan(t)

                                            where n ∈ Z.
