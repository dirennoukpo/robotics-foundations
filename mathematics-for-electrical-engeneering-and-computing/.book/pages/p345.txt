                              Each of the terms on the right-hand side of this definition is of the form
                              ai bj where i and j are different choices of the numbers 1 and 2. We
                              can define higher order determinants by using ideas of permutations.
                              We notice that the term a1 b2 above has a positive sign because the indices
                              1 and 2 appear in order, whereas the term a2 b1 has a negative sign because
                              the indices 2,1 are reversed.
