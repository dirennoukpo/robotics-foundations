                                        Example 21.4 What is the probability on one throw of a die getting a
                                        number less than 3?
                                        Solution Give this event the name A. Then A = {1, 2} and S =
                                        {1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6}. The number in A is 2 and the number in S is 6. There-
                                        fore, as each outcomes is equally likely, p(A), the probability of A is
                                        2/6 = 1/3.
