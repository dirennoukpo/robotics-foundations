            The inverse of a matrix using
            (Adjoint(A))/|A|
            We have already seen how to find the inverse of a matrix by using
            elimination. It is also possible to find the inverse by the following
            procedure:
            (1)     Find the matrix of minors.
            (2)     Multiply the minor for row i and column j by (−1)i+j . This is then
                    called the matrix of cofactors.
