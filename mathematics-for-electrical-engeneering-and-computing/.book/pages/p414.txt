            The first two terms in this expression are caused by the non-zero initial
            values and decay exponentially. The next two terms also decay with
            increasing t and are as a result of the abrupt turn on of the input at t = 0.
            Thus for a stable system, all four terms are part of the transient solution
            that dies out as t increases. The fifth and final term is the sinusoidal input
            f (t) = ejωt multiplied by H ( jω). H ( jω) is a complex constant. This is
            the steady state response of the system to a single sinusoidal input, which
            we have shown in this case is given by

            Y (t) = H ( jω)ejωt .

            We can then see that for a single sinusoid input the steady state response
            is found by substituting s = jω into the transfer function for the system
