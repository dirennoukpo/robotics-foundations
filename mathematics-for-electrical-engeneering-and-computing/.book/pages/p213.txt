                                    Example 9.16       Show that (1, −1) and (−2, −2) are at right angles.
                                    Solution
                                    (1, −1) · (−2, −2) = (1)(−2) + (−1)(−2) = −2 + 2 = 0
                                    Hence, they are at right angles.
